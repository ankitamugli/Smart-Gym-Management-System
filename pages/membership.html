Smart membership and auto renewal

See your plan, choose how it renews, and compare what each plan includes.

Payment screens are a demonstration. No money is charged, no card is stored (only the last 4 digits of a test card are kept in this browser), and nothing renews automatically. Real payments and automatic renewals need a secure backend with a payment gateway (such as Razorpay or Stripe), server-side tokenised cards, webhooks and scheduled renewal jobs. Never collect or store full card numbers in frontend code.
