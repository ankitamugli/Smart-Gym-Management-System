Demo projection, not an AI prediction. The dashed line is a straight-line trend drawn from your recent weight entries by a few lines of JavaScript in this browser. It does not know about your diet, sleep, training or health, and real bodies do not change in straight lines. A true predictor needs a trained model on a server behind GET /progress/prediction. Treat the dates as a rough illustration only.
