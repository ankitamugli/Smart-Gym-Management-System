How check-in will work with a backend

  1. App asks GET /checkins/qr-token for a short-lived signed token.
  2. The QR code shows that token.
  3. The desk scanner (or staff app) sends it to POST /checkins.
  4. The server verifies the signature, expiry and active membership, then records the visit.
